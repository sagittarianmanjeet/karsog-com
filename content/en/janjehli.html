The valley

Janjehli lies on the far side of the Shikari Devi ridge, in the Seraj area of Mandi district. Its slopes are covered in deodar and spruce forest, apple orchards and villages of wood-and-stone houses, and the air is cool even in summer. It is the last real market on this side of the mountain, with shops, a hospital and places to stay.

Most visitors come here for Shikari Devi. Many stay a night in Janjehli and go up to the temple at first light, when the views are clearest.

How to reach

  • From Karsog, in the open season: the shortest way is over the Shikari Devi road, about 27 km (1½ hours). It is a narrow mountain road and is usually closed by snow in winter.
  • When that road is closed: the way round via Chhatri is about 84 km, 3½–4 hours.
  • To Shikari Devi: a road of about 16–18 km climbs from Janjehli to the parking below the temple. Local jeeps and taxis make the trip; ask in the market.

Where to stay and eat

  • Small hotels in and around the market, with simple rooms.
  • PWD rest house, at government rates, subject to availability.
  • Homestays, often with home-cooked Himachali food; ask locally.

Good to know

  • Fill up with fuel and carry some cash before you leave Karsog.
  • Civil Hospital Janjehli: 01907-256512. More numbers on useful numbers.
  • Mobile signal is weak outside town. Download offline maps before you go.
  • December to March brings snow; check the weather and ask about the roads before travelling.
How far is Janjehli from Karsog? About 27 km over the Shikari Devi road, around 1½ hours by car, when that road is open. In winter, when it is usually closed by snow, the way round via Chhatri is about 84 km. How far is Shikari Devi from Janjehli? About 16–18 km by road to the parking below the temple, followed by a steep flight of steps. Where can I stay in Janjehli? Janjehli has small hotels, a PWD rest house and homestays. When is the best time to visit Janjehli? April to June and September to November. Winters are snowy and the monsoon brings landslides on the hill roads.
