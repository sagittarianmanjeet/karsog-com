FAQ

Questions travellers
ask us

What is the first bus from Mandi to Karsog?

HRTC online booking lists one Mandi to Karsog service, at 4:30 AM (₹285, about 5¾ hours; checked 25 Sep 2026). Local timings put the first ordinary bus around 6:00 AM, arriving about 9:00 AM. Confirm at the Mandi bus stand.

What is the last bus from Karsog to Mandi?

By the timetable board at Karsog bus stand (photographed 25 Sep 2026), the last direct bus to Mandi leaves at 5:15 PM. Other Mandi buses: 9:30 AM, 11:10 AM (via Sorta, also bookable online) and 2:00 PM (Reckong Peo bus via Sorta). Confirm at the stand.

How far is Karsog from Shimla?

About 106 km by road from Shimla ISBT (Google Maps), roughly a 4-hour drive via Naldehra and Tattapani. HRTC online booking lists buses at 6:30 AM and 2:40 PM, fare ₹278, about 5–5½ hours.

What is the best time to visit Karsog?

March to November. Apple season (September–October) is especially beautiful. Winters (December–February) can bring snow and road disruptions.

Are the roads safe in the monsoon?

During July–September, landslides and slow patches are possible, especially on the Shimla–Tattapani side. In heavy rain, check the road-status banner above and call the Karsog Control Room (+91 93172-07043).

Are there ATMs and petrol pumps in Karsog?

Karsog main market has bank branches, ATMs and a petrol pump. Carry cash — villages and trek routes (Shikari Devi, Kamru Nag) have neither ATMs nor fuel.

Is there mobile network in Karsog?

Jio, Airtel and BSNL generally work in town. Signal is weak or absent on the Shikari Devi and Kamru Nag trails — download offline maps before trekking.

Where should I stay in Karsog?

The HPTDC Mamleshwar Resort at Chindi (15 km), small hotels in the main market, PWD rest houses and registered homestays. Book ahead in apple season (September–October).
