Temple

Mahunag Temple

One of the most revered temples in Karsog Valley, dedicated to Lord Karna of the Mahabharata — said to have manifested here after Karna's last rites at nearby Tattapani. Especially sought for guidance on legal disputes. The annual Mool Mahunag fair (Sankranti) draws thousands. Full guide →

Temple · Heritage

Mamleshwar Mahadev

A 7th-century Shiva temple 2 km from Karsog bazaar housing three extraordinary artefacts: a 5,000-year-old grain of wheat, a 6-foot drum of rhinoceros hide said to have been played by Bhima, and an Akhand Dhuna — an eternal sacred fire that has never been extinguished. Full guide →

Temple · Pagoda

Kamaksha Devi · Kao

A striking pagoda-style temple at Kao village — the royal deity of the Suket kingdom. Grand chariot processions during Durga Ashtami. The pagoda architecture and hilltop location make it one of Karsog's most photographed temples.
