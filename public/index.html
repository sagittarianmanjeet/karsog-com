About Karsog

A valley of old wooden temples, terraced fields and apple orchards in the south of Mandi district, where every village has its own devta.

This guide is made in Karsog, by a local. Bus times are copied from the timetable board at the bus stand, fair dates are checked against past years, and every photo on the site was taken over the valley by drone.

FAQ

Questions travellers ask

What is the first bus from Mandi to Karsog?
HRTC online booking lists one Mandi to Karsog service, at 4:30 AM (₹285, about 5¾ hours; checked 25 Sep 2026). Local timings put the first ordinary bus around 6:00 AM, arriving about 9:00 AM. Confirm at the Mandi bus stand. All Mandi ⇄ Karsog buses.
What is the last bus from Karsog to Mandi?
By the timetable board at Karsog bus stand (photographed 25 Sep 2026), the last direct bus to Mandi leaves at 5:15 PM. Other Mandi buses leave at 9:30 AM, 11:10 AM (via Sorta, also bookable online) and 2:00 PM (the Reckong Peo bus, via Sorta). Confirm at the stand.
How far is Karsog from Shimla?
About 95 km by road from Shimla ISBT (Tutikandi) to Karsog bus stand, via Tattapani: about 4 hours by car. HRTC buses take about 5 to 5½ hours. Distances to other places.
What is the best time to visit Karsog?
March to November. May brings the Mahunag Mela, and the apple season (September–October) is especially beautiful. In winter (December–February) the higher roads can close for snow.
Does it snow in Karsog?
Karsog town is at about 1,404 m, so snow in town is occasional and usually light, in the coldest weeks of winter. Shikari Devi, Chindi and Janjehli get heavier snow and their roads can close. Live weather and snow forecast.
Are the roads safe in the monsoon?
From July to September landslides and slow patches are common, especially on the Shimla–Tattapani side. In heavy rain, check the weather page and call the Karsog Control Room (+91 93172-07043) before you set out.
Are there ATMs and petrol pumps in Karsog?
Yes, Karsog town has bank branches, ATMs and a petrol pump. Carry cash for villages and treks (Shikari Devi, Kamru Nag), which have neither.
Is there mobile network in Karsog?
Jio, Airtel and BSNL generally work in town. Signal is weak or missing on the Shikari Devi and Kamru Nag trails, so download offline maps first.
Where can I stay in Karsog?
The HPTDC Hotel Mamleshwar at Chindi (about 7 km), small hotels in Karsog town, the PWD rest house and a few homestays. Book ahead in apple season and during big fairs. Hotels in Karsog.
