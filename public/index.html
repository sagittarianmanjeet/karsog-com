What to See

Sacred peaks, ancient
temples, hidden trails.

From 7th-century Shiva shrines to high-altitude lakes and Kath-Kuni heritage forts — Karsog Valley holds far more than most travellers expect.

Temple

Mahunag Temple

One of the most revered temples in Karsog Valley, dedicated to Lord Karna of the Mahabharata — said to have manifested here after Karna's last rites at nearby Tattapani. Especially sought for guidance on legal disputes. The annual Mool Mahunag fair (Sankranti) draws thousands. Full guide →

Temple · Heritage

Mamleshwar Mahadev

A 7th-century Shiva temple 2 km from Karsog bazaar housing three extraordinary artefacts: a 5,000-year-old grain of wheat, a 6-foot drum of rhinoceros hide said to have been played by Bhima, and an Akhand Dhuna — an eternal sacred fire that has never been extinguished. Full guide →

Temple · Pagoda

Kamaksha Devi · Kao

A striking pagoda-style temple at Kao village — the royal deity of the Suket kingdom. Grand chariot processions during Durga Ashtami. The pagoda architecture and hilltop location make it one of Karsog's most photographed temples.

Lake · Trek

Kamru Nag Lake

A sacred high-altitude lake (3,334 m) and temple dedicated to Kamru Nag, the rain god. Devotees throw coins and ornaments into the lake — accumulated over centuries. An easy-to-moderate trek with stunning panoramic valley views. Full guide →

Heritage

Pangna Fort

Built around 1211 AD in Kath-Kuni style (layered wood and stone), this 7-story, 60-foot tower was the former capital of the Suket dynasty. The alternating wood-stone construction gives earthquake resilience. Top floor houses the Mahamaya Devi Temple. Full guide →

Trek · Temple

Shikari Devi

A moderate 5–6 km climb from Janjehli through thick deodar and spruce forests to a peak at 3,359 m. The roofless summit temple is famous for the legend that snow never settles inside the shrine. Spectacular 360° Himalayan views. Full guide →

Getting Here

How to reach
Karsog Valley

🚗

From Mandi

~114 km · 3–4 hrs via Chail Chowk & Rohanda. Regular HRTC buses from Mandi bus stand. Shared taxis also available. Road in good condition most of the year.

🏞️

From Shimla

~106 km · 4–5 hrs via Naldehra & Tattapani. HRTC direct buses available. Beautiful Sutlej valley views en route. Best done in morning to arrive before dark.

✈️

Nearest Airport

Bhuntar (Kullu) — ~110 km. Chandigarh (~220 km) is the larger alternative with daily flights. Taxi or HRTC bus onwards from either.

🚉

Nearest Railway

Kalka broad gauge (~160 km) — most accessible by rail. Jogindernagar narrow gauge (~80 km). Mandi has no railway.

Plan Your Trip

Ready-made
itineraries

Three simple plans using local taxis and buses — temple circuits, hot springs and the big treks. Costs are indicative.

1 Day

🛕 Temple Circuit

Morning at Mamleshwar Mahadev (2 km from bazaar) → Kamaksha Devi at Kao → Mahunag Temple → evening at Karsog bazaar with a dhaba dinner.

Taxi ~₹1,500–2,500 (Dzire, full day)
1 Day

♨️ Tattapani & Chindi

Soak in the Sutlej-side hot springs at Tattapani, return via the Chindi sunset point and HPTDC café. Loveliest in the colder months.

Taxi ~₹1,200–1,800 (Dzire)
2 Days

🥾 The Big Treks

Day 1 — drive to Janjehli, trek Shikari Devi (5–6 km), overnight at Janjehli or a homestay. Day 2 — Kamru Nag lake trek, back in Karsog by evening.

Ertiga/Innova ~₹2,000–2,500 per day
Stay & Eat

Where to sleep,
where to eat

A small town with honest, simple options — government rest houses, family-run hotels, dhabas serving siddu and dham, and a handful of homestays. Book ahead in apple season.

🛏 Where to Stay

HPTDC Mamleshwar Resort

Government Hotel · Chindi (15 km)

Reliable HPTDC property with valley views, comfortable rooms and an in-house restaurant. Good value, especially off-season.

Private Hotels · Karsog

Karsog Main Market

Several small hotels and guesthouses including Divine Valley. Hot water and attached bathrooms standard.

PWD Rest House

Chindi & Pangna · Govt

Clean, basic accommodation with historic charm at government rates. Civilians can book through the SDM office subject to availability.

Homestays

Various villages

Authentic Himachali hospitality with home-cooked food. Sarvpoornam Villa in Pangna is good for groups. Ask the SDM office for registered homestays.

🍲 Where to Eat

Local Dhabas

Main Bazaar

Hearty rajma-chawal, kadhi, parathas and chai. Cheapest, freshest, and often the best food in town.

Siddu & Babru

Try at festivals

Steamed wheat dumplings stuffed with poppy seeds or walnuts — Karsog's signature. Look for them at temple fairs and homestays.

Dham

By appointment

The traditional Himachali community feast — served on leaf plates. Ask homestays to arrange one with notice.

Hotel Restaurants

Main Market

Larger hotels serve a wider menu including Chinese and North Indian. Sit-down meals after a long trek.

Fairs & Festivals

A year in
the valley

Dates follow the Hindu calendar and shift each year — confirm locally before planning around them.

Feb – Mar
Maha Shivratri — special worship and crowds at Mamleshwar Mahadev.
Mar – Apr
Chaitra Navratri — Kamaksha Devi (Kao) processions and temple celebrations.
Mid-April
Mool Mahunag Fair on Baisakhi Sankranti — the valley's biggest fair, drawing thousands.
June
Kamrunag Mela (approx 13–15 June) — thousands trek to the sacred lake.
Sep – Oct
Shardiya Navratri & apple harvest — orchards at their best, the bazaar at its busiest.
Nov – Dec
Budhi Diwali in some villages · first snow on Shikari Devi.

FAQ

Questions travellers
ask us

What is the first bus from Mandi to Karsog?

HRTC online booking lists one Mandi to Karsog service, at 4:30 AM (₹285, about 5¾ hours; checked 25 Sep 2026). Local timings put the first ordinary bus around 6:00 AM, arriving about 9:00 AM. Confirm at the Mandi bus stand.

What is the last bus from Karsog to Mandi?

By the timetable board at Karsog bus stand (photographed 25 Sep 2026), the last direct bus to Mandi leaves at 5:15 PM. Other Mandi buses: 9:30 AM, 11:10 AM (via Sorta, also bookable online) and 2:00 PM (Reckong Peo bus via Sorta). Confirm at the stand.

How far is Karsog from Shimla?

About 106 km by road from Shimla ISBT (Google Maps), roughly a 4-hour drive via Naldehra and Tattapani. HRTC online booking lists buses at 6:30 AM and 2:40 PM, fare ₹278, about 5–5½ hours.

What is the best time to visit Karsog?

March to November. Apple season (September–October) is especially beautiful. Winters (December–February) can bring snow and road disruptions.

Are the roads safe in the monsoon?

During July–September, landslides and slow patches are possible, especially on the Shimla–Tattapani side. In heavy rain, check the road-status banner above and call the Karsog Control Room (+91 93172-07043).

Are there ATMs and petrol pumps in Karsog?

Karsog main market has bank branches, ATMs and a petrol pump. Carry cash — villages and trek routes (Shikari Devi, Kamru Nag) have neither ATMs nor fuel.

Is there mobile network in Karsog?

Jio, Airtel and BSNL generally work in town. Signal is weak or absent on the Shikari Devi and Kamru Nag trails — download offline maps before trekking.

Where should I stay in Karsog?

The HPTDC Mamleshwar Resort at Chindi (15 km), small hotels in the main market, PWD rest houses and registered homestays. Book ahead in apple season (September–October).
