Frequently asked

What time is the first bus from Mandi to Karsog?

HRTC online booking lists one Mandi to Karsog service, at 4:30 AM (₹285, about 5¾ hours; checked 25 Sep 2026). Local timings put the first ordinary bus around 6:00 AM, arriving about 9:00 AM. Confirm at the Mandi bus stand.

What if I miss the 3:00 PM last bus from Mandi?

Options: a shared taxi (leaves when full), a private taxi (₹9+/km, roughly ₹1,100–1,500 to Karsog), or overnight in Mandi and the 6:00 AM bus.

Is the road okay for people who get motion sickness?

It's a winding hill road for most of the 3–4 hours. Sit near the front, keep the window cracked open, and avoid heavy meals before boarding.

Do buses run in winter and monsoon?

Yes, but snow (Dec–Feb on high stretches) and monsoon landslides (Jul–Sep) can delay or cancel services. Always call ahead in those months.
