Shimla ⇄ Karsog
bus timings

The ~106 km road from Shimla to Karsog via Naldehra and Tattapani is one of Himachal's prettiest bus rides — Sutlej valley views most of the way. Timings are approximate and change seasonally: confirm at the ISBT or bus stand the evening before.

Golden rule of hill buses: timings shift with season, weather and road works. Call ahead, arrive 20 minutes early, and in the monsoon check the road-status banner on the homepage — the Tattapani side develops slow patches in heavy rain.

Distances by road

To Karsog bus stand, by car (Google Maps, checked 25 Sep 2026): from Mandi bus stand ~114 km (about 4 hrs) · from Shimla ISBT Tutikandi ~106 km (about 4 hrs) · from Chail Chowk ~87 km (about 3½ hrs). Buses take longer.

The route

The bus climbs out of Shimla past Naldehra (golf course and deodar forest), drops to the Sutlej at Tattapani — the hot springs make a fine break in the journey — then climbs again through Chindi's orchards to Karsog at 1,404 m. Morning departure is best: you arrive before dark with the whole valley ahead of you.

Alternatives

Taxi: a one-way cab from Shimla runs at outstation rates (from about ₹9/km — negotiate the total before starting; see the rate card). The drive is 4–5 hours with stops at Tattapani and Chindi possible.

From Mandi instead: if you're coming from the Kullu–Mandi side, see the Mandi–Karsog bus timings — 4 daily departures.

Frequently asked

Can I book the Shimla–Karsog bus online?

Some services, yes. HRTC's booking site (online.hrtchp.com) listed the 6:30 AM and 2:40 PM departures on 25 Sep 2026. Other ordinary buses are first-come-first-served at the stand.

Is the road safe in the monsoon?

July–September can bring landslides and slow patches on the Tattapani side. Buses run unless the road closes — check locally and see the homepage road banner.

Where does the bus stop for food?

Typically a halt near Tattapani or Chindi — dhabas with tea, parathas and thalis. Carry water and snacks in any case.

Can I break the journey at Tattapani?

Yes — many travellers hop off at Tattapani for the hot springs and continue on a later bus or shared taxi, but confirm onward timings first; services are few.
