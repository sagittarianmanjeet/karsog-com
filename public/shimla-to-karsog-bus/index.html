FAQ

Common questions

What time is the bus from Shimla to Karsog?
HRTC online booking lists Shimla ISBT to Karsog buses at 6:30 AM and 2:40 PM (₹278; checked 25 Sep 2026). Manohar Bus Service (private) leaves Shimla ISBT at 11:00 AM.
What time is the first bus from Karsog to Shimla?
The private Manohar bus leaves at 4:40 AM, and the first HRTC bus on the Karsog board leaves at 5:00 AM (via Bagshad, also bookable online).
How far is Karsog from Shimla?
About 94 km by road from Shimla ISBT via Tattapani, about 3¾ hours by car. Buses take about 5 to 5½ hours.
Can I break the journey at Tattapani?
Yes. Many travellers stop at Tattapani for the hot springs and continue on a later bus, but services are few, so check the onward time first.
