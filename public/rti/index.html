CAG audit06.08.2026

PWD Karsog Division — CAG inspection report 2023-24

Principal Accountant General (Audit), Himachal Pradesh · Period: July 2021 – June 2023 · Supplied by email under RTI, letter dated 06.08.2026

What the document says

  • ₹16.33 crore (₹1,632.74 lakh) spent on Scheduled Castes Sub Plan link roads that were still incomplete — audit calls it unfruitful expenditure (Para 3).
  • ₹15.23 crore spent on the combined office building at Karsog, still incomplete — unproductive expenditure. The same para records a ₹8.06 crore liability and ₹7.43 crore cost escalation from the time overrun (Para 2).
  • ₹3.74 crore spent on the ITI Karsog building — unfruitful expenditure (Para 1).
  • ₹2.35 crore (₹234.83 lakh) of deposits lying unclaimed since 1998-99 not credited to government revenue, as the rules require (Para 22).
  • ₹1.62 crore labour welfare cess not deposited with the HP Building & Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (Para 6).
  • ₹1.03 crore compensation not levied on contractors for 38 delayed works — audit calls it undue favour to contractors. The Executive Engineer confirmed the facts and figures (Para 25).
More findings in this report (4)
  • ₹1.49 crore of work split into smaller agreements (Para 11).
  • Contractor bills worth ₹7.09 crore in 331 works not finalised (Para 24).
  • ₹3.69 crore of government funds blocked on deposit work (Para 10).
  • Road Safety Audit of roads not conducted (Para 13).

Keep in mind: Inspection report paras are audit observations. The division replies to them, and a para is settled once the auditors accept the reply. The current status of each para can be asked from the AG office.

Forest Division Karsog — seized timber not disposed of

CAG Audit Report for the year ended 31 March 2016, Revenue Sector (Report No. 1 of 2017), Chapter VI, Para 6.3

What the document says

  • Timber seized between 2011-12 and 2014-15 was lying in depots: Karsog 61.241 cu.m worth ₹29.33 lakh, out of 539 cu.m worth ₹2.79 crore across 12 divisions.
  • No record showed whether the DFOs had taken steps or obtained court orders to dispose of it. A 1999 instruction of the Principal Chief Conservator asked for court orders to auction such produce within 15 days.
  • Non-disposal blocks revenue and adds watch-and-ward cost while the timber deteriorates.
  • DFOs Nachan, Mandi and Theog replied to audit. The remaining DFOs, Karsog among them, had not furnished any reply.

How to file your own RTI

It costs ₹10 and can be done from a phone.

Himachal government offices

Nagar Panchayat, PWD, Jal Shakti, HPSEBL, HRTC, Tehsil, BDO, schools, colleges, State Audit.

onlinerti.hp.gov.in ↗

Central government offices

AG / CAG audit office, RBI, banks, post office, BSNL, railways, national highways.

rtionline.gov.in ↗
  1. Pick the right portal above and register with your email and mobile number.
  2. Choose the department and office. Write what you want clearly, point by point. Ask for PDF by email — Section 7(9) says information should be given in the form asked for.
  3. Pay the ₹10 fee online. BPL card holders pay no fee (Section 7(5)); upload the BPL certificate.
  4. The office must reply within 30 days (Section 7(1)). If the reply comes late, the information must be given free of cost (Section 7(6)).
  5. No reply or an incomplete reply? File a first appeal on the same portal within 30 days (Section 19(1)).
  6. Still not satisfied? File a second appeal with the State Information Commission (state offices) or the Central Information Commission (central offices) within 90 days (Section 19(3)).

In our experience in 2026, the Himachal portal accepted only about 500 characters of text and did not reliably save attachments — keep requests short and put everything in the text box.

Frequently asked

Is it legal to publish these documents?

Yes. They were supplied by public offices under the RTI Act, 2005, or published by the audit department. The Act puts no restriction on how a citizen uses information once it has been supplied. We have removed the applicant’s phone number, email and address.

Does an audit objection mean money was stolen?

No. An audit para records what auditors found in the files — missing records, rules not followed, money lying unused. The office gets to explain, and paras are dropped once audit accepts the reply. Some paras point to serious problems; others are settled with paperwork. Read them as questions the office has to answer.

How do I get the latest audit report of my panchayat or office?

Use one of the copy-ready texts above. Gram Panchayats, Nagar Panchayats and college funds are audited by the HP State Audit Department. Most other government offices are audited by the AG (Audit) Himachal Pradesh, which is part of the CAG.

Can I use these documents in my own RTI, complaint or appeal?

Yes. Quote the letter number and date printed on the document. It also shows the office that the same kind of information has already been released.

Why are some parts blacked out?

Only the applicant’s personal contact details — phone number, email and home address — have been covered. Nothing else in the documents has been changed.
