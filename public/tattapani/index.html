Hot springs

Tattapani

“Tatta pani” means hot water. Natural hot springs rise beside the Sutlej here, halfway between Karsog and Shimla, and every January thousands come for a holy dip at the Makar Sankranti fair.

The hot springs

Hot, sulphur-rich water comes up along the banks of the Sutlej at Tattapani, and bathing here is an old tradition. Many people believe the water eases joint pain and skin problems. It is most inviting from October to March, when the air is cold and the valley fills with winter sun.

An honest note: after the Kol Dam reservoir raised the level of the Sutlej, most of the old open springs on the river bank went under water. Today bathing is mostly in tanks and pools run by guesthouses and resorts, usually for a small fee. Ask locally about the current arrangements.

Lohri and Makar Sankranti

Tattapani’s biggest days are 13–15 January, for Lohri and Makar Sankranti. Thousands come for a holy dip, and the river bank fills with stalls, swings and cultural evenings. Huge pots of khichdi are cooked and shared. If you go, arrive early: the road and the parking fill quickly. See the melas calendar.

The legend of Karna

Tattapani is part of the valley’s best-known story: people believe Karna’s last rites were performed here on the Sutlej, and that he later appeared in the hills above as the devta Mahunag.

How to reach

  • From Karsog: about 46 km, 1¾ hours by car. HRTC buses from Karsog to Shimla run via Tattapani; see the Shimla ⇄ Karsog buses.
  • From Shimla: about 50 km, one of the most popular short drives out of Shimla.
  • Rafting: in season, rafting runs on the Sutlej here. It depends on the water level and the operators, so confirm locally.

Good to know

  • In the monsoon (July–September) the roads along the Sutlej can have landslides and slow stretches. Check the weather before you set out.
  • Tattapani has riverside guesthouses and resorts, but many visitors come as a day trip from Karsog or Shimla.

FAQ

Common questions

How far is Tattapani from Karsog?
About 46 km by road, about 1¾ hours by car. HRTC buses between Karsog and Shimla run via Tattapani.
Can you bathe in the Tattapani hot springs?
Yes, but since the Kol Dam reservoir covered most of the old open springs, bathing is mostly in tanks and pools at guesthouses and resorts, usually for a fee. Ask locally about current arrangements.
When is the Tattapani mela?
On Lohri and Makar Sankranti, usually 13–15 January, with a holy dip in the hot springs, stalls and cultural programmes.
What is the best time to visit Tattapani?
October to March, when the hot water is most enjoyable. Avoid the monsoon months if you can.
