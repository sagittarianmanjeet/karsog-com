Getting Here

How to reach
Karsog Valley

🚗

From Mandi

~114 km · 3–4 hrs via Chail Chowk & Rohanda. Regular HRTC buses from Mandi bus stand. Shared taxis also available. Road in good condition most of the year.

🏞️

From Shimla

~106 km · 4–5 hrs via Naldehra & Tattapani. HRTC direct buses available. Beautiful Sutlej valley views en route. Best done in morning to arrive before dark.

✈️

Nearest Airport

Bhuntar (Kullu) — ~110 km. Chandigarh (~220 km) is the larger alternative with daily flights. Taxi or HRTC bus onwards from either.

🚉

Nearest Railway

Kalka broad gauge (~160 km) — most accessible by rail. Jogindernagar narrow gauge (~80 km). Mandi has no railway.
