Chindi

A quiet hillside of apple orchards and deodar just above Karsog, with a carved goddess temple, wide sunset views and the valley’s government-run HPTDC hotel.

7 km from KarsogChindi Mata Mela, 2–4 AugustHPTDC Hotel Mamleshwar
Directions

Why go to Chindi

Chindi sits on a cool, orchard-covered hillside above the Karsog valley. It is the kind of place people stop at for an hour and stay at for a day: the evening light over the hills is lovely, and in September–October the apple orchards are at their best, with fruit sold straight from the trees.

Chindi Mata temple

The beautifully carved temple of Chindi Mata stands among the orchards and deodar trees. Local people say the goddess showed a priest the plan of her temple through a line of ants, and that the name Chindi comes from chinti, the word for ant.

The Chindi Mata Mela is held every year on 2–4 August. See the melas calendar.

Where to stay

The HPTDC Hotel Mamleshwar, run by Himachal Tourism, is the best-known place to stay in the area, with valley views and a restaurant. It is named after the Mamleshwar Mahadev temple in Karsog. Book ahead in apple season and around the August mela. See all hotels near Karsog.

How to reach

  • From Karsog: about 7 km by the short, steep road (about 25 minutes), or about 15 km by the main road (about 35 minutes).
  • By taxi: taxis wait at the Karsog taxi stand in the main market; agree the fare before you start.
  • Day out: combine Chindi with the Tattapani hot springs, or with the temples of Mamleshwar and Kao.

Good to know

  • Chindi is higher and colder than Karsog town. It sometimes gets snow between December and February; check the weather in winter.
  • The temple is a working shrine: dress modestly and take off your shoes.

FAQ

Common questions

How far is Chindi from Karsog?
About 7 km by the short, steep road (about 25 minutes), or about 15 km by the main road (about 35 minutes).
When is the Chindi Mata Mela?
Every year on 2–4 August, at the Chindi Mata temple.
Is there a hotel at Chindi?
Yes, the HPTDC Hotel Mamleshwar, run by Himachal Tourism, with a restaurant and valley views. Book ahead in apple season.
Does it snow in Chindi?
Sometimes, between December and February. Check the weather and road conditions before driving up in winter.
