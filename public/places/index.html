FAQ

Common questions

What are the best places to visit in Karsog?
The Mahunag temple, Shikari Devi on the highest peak of Mandi district, Mamleshwar Mahadev at Mamel, Kamaksha Devi at Kao, the Kamru Nag lake trek, the Tattapani hot springs, Pangna fort, Chindi and the Janjehli valley. Each has a full guide on this site.
Which places near Karsog can I see in one day?
Mamleshwar Mahadev, Kamaksha Devi at Kao and Mahunag make a one-day temple circuit. Chindi and Tattapani also fit in a day. Shikari Devi and Janjehli are best with a night in Janjehli.
