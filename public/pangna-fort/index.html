Heritage

Pangna fort

Before the Suket kings ruled from Sundernagar, Pangna was their seat. Their tall tower of wood and stone still stands in the village, with the Mahamaya goddess temple on its top floor, in daily worship.

19 km from KarsogMahamaya temple on topKatal Mela, early June
Directions

The first seat of Suket

Pangna was an early capital of the old Suket kingdom, which once ruled this part of Mandi. Local histories date its fort to around 1211 AD. The capital later moved away, but the tower, its temple and the old houses around it kept Pangna the cultural heart of the area.

The top floor of the tower is the temple of the goddess Mahamaya, still in daily worship. That is why the tower has never been abandoned.

Built in kath-kuni

The fort is built in the traditional kath-kuni style of the hills: layers of stone laid between long beams of deodar wood. The timber lets the walls flex instead of cracking, and buildings like this have survived earthquakes that brought down newer ones. Pangna’s tower is one of the finest examples left in the area.

Katal Mela

Pangna’s local fair, the Katal Mela, is held in early June, usually around 8–11 June. See the melas calendar.

How to reach

  • From Karsog: about 19 km, 50 minutes by car on an ordinary hill road that is fine for small cars in dry weather.
  • By bus: buses on the Karsog–Pangna–Mandi line stop at Pangna; see the Karsog bus timetable.
  • Stay: Pangna has a PWD rest house and homestays, but most visitors come on a day trip from Karsog.

Good to know

  • The temple floor is an active shrine, and access depends on the priests and caretakers. Ask politely in the village.
  • Dress modestly and take off your shoes at the temple level. Photos of the outside are fine; ask before taking any inside.
  • Walk around the old village too; its houses are built in the same style.

FAQ

Common questions

How far is Pangna from Karsog?
About 19 km by road, around 50 minutes by car. Buses on the Karsog–Pangna–Mandi line stop at Pangna.
How old is Pangna fort?
Local histories date it to around 1211 AD, when Pangna was a seat of the Suket kings.
What is at the top of Pangna fort?
The temple of the goddess Mahamaya, still in daily worship.
Is there an entry fee at Pangna fort?
No. The fort and temple are free to visit; offerings are voluntary. Access to the temple floor depends on the priests.
