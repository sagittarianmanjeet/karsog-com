Snow in Karsog

Karsog town sits at about 1,404 m, so snow in town is occasional and usually light, in the coldest weeks of winter. Higher places such as Shikari Devi (about 3,359 m), Chindi and Janjehli get heavier snow, and the road over Shikari Devi usually closes for the winter.

FAQ

Common questions

Does it snow in Karsog?
Karsog town is at about 1,404 m, so snow in town is occasional and usually light, in the coldest weeks of winter. Shikari Devi, Chindi and Janjehli get heavier snow and their roads can close.
How high is Karsog?
Karsog town is about 1,404 metres (4,606 feet) above sea level. Shikari Devi, the highest point nearby, is about 3,359 m.
What is the best weather to visit Karsog?
Spring (March–May) and autumn (September–November) are mild and clear. July and August bring heavy monsoon rain, and winters are cold with snow higher up.
