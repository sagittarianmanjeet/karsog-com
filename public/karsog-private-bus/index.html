FAQ

Common questions

Which private buses run from Karsog?
Private operators on Karsog routes include Ayush Nipun Bus Service, Chetan Bus Service, Lajhari Bus Service, Manohar Bus Service, Radhika Bus Service, Sharma Bus Service, Sheetla Bus Service, VIP Coach. They run to Shimla, Sundernagar, Mandi, Hamirpur, Rampur, Ani and nearby villages. Timings can change, so confirm before travelling.
What is the first private bus from Karsog to Shimla?
Manohar Bus Service leaves Karsog for Shimla ISBT at 4:40 AM, with a second bus at 7:30 AM. Timings can change, so confirm before travelling.
Is there a private bus from Karsog to Sundernagar or Mandi?
Yes. VIP Coach runs Karsog–Sundernagar–Mandi at 4:40 AM and 6:00 AM, and to Sundernagar at 7:10 AM; Sheetla and Chetan also run to Sundernagar. See the tables on this page.
