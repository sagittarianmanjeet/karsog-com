Plan Your Trip

Ready-made
itineraries

Three simple plans using local taxis and buses — temple circuits, hot springs and the big treks. Costs are indicative.

1 Day

🛕 Temple Circuit

Morning at Mamleshwar Mahadev (2 km from bazaar) → Kamaksha Devi at Kao → Mahunag Temple → evening at Karsog bazaar with a dhaba dinner.

Taxi ~₹1,500–2,500 (Dzire, full day)
1 Day

♨️ Tattapani & Chindi

Soak in the Sutlej-side hot springs at Tattapani, return via the Chindi sunset point and HPTDC café. Loveliest in the colder months.

Taxi ~₹1,200–1,800 (Dzire)
2 Days

🥾 The Big Treks

Day 1 — drive to Janjehli, trek Shikari Devi (5–6 km), overnight at Janjehli or a homestay. Day 2 — Kamru Nag lake trek, back in Karsog by evening.

Ertiga/Innova ~₹2,000–2,500 per day
Stay & Eat

Where to sleep,
where to eat

A small town with honest, simple options — government rest houses, family-run hotels, dhabas serving siddu and dham, and a handful of homestays. Book ahead in apple season.

🛏 Where to Stay

HPTDC Mamleshwar Resort

Government Hotel · Chindi (15 km)

Reliable HPTDC property with valley views, comfortable rooms and an in-house restaurant. Good value, especially off-season.

Private Hotels · Karsog

Karsog Main Market

Several small hotels and guesthouses including Divine Valley. Hot water and attached bathrooms standard.

PWD Rest House

Chindi & Pangna · Govt

Clean, basic accommodation with historic charm at government rates. Civilians can book through the SDM office subject to availability.

Homestays

Various villages

Authentic Himachali hospitality with home-cooked food. Sarvpoornam Villa in Pangna is good for groups. Ask the SDM office for registered homestays.

🍲 Where to Eat

Local Dhabas

Main Bazaar

Hearty rajma-chawal, kadhi, parathas and chai. Cheapest, freshest, and often the best food in town.

Siddu & Babru

Try at festivals

Steamed wheat dumplings stuffed with poppy seeds or walnuts — Karsog's signature. Look for them at temple fairs and homestays.

Dham

By appointment

The traditional Himachali community feast — served on leaf plates. Ask homestays to arrange one with notice.

Hotel Restaurants

Main Market

Larger hotels serve a wider menu including Chinese and North Indian. Sit-down meals after a long trek.

Fairs & Festivals

A year in
the valley

Dates follow the Hindu calendar and shift each year — confirm locally before planning around them.

Feb – Mar
Maha Shivratri — special worship and crowds at Mamleshwar Mahadev.
Mar – Apr
Chaitra Navratri — Kamaksha Devi (Kao) processions and temple celebrations.
Mid-April
Mool Mahunag Fair on Baisakhi Sankranti — the valley's biggest fair, drawing thousands.
June
Kamrunag Mela (approx 13–15 June) — thousands trek to the sacred lake.
Sep – Oct
Shardiya Navratri & apple harvest — orchards at their best, the bazaar at its busiest.
Nov – Dec
Budhi Diwali in some villages · first snow on Shikari Devi.
