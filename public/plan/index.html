Getting here

How to reach Karsog

Road distances from Karsog bus stand by the fastest route on Google Maps, checked on 28 Sep 2026. Hill roads are slow; buses take longer than cars.

From ShimlaAbout 94 km from ISBT Tutikandi via Tattapani, around 3 h 40 min by car. HRTC buses take about 5 to 5½ hours. Shimla ⇄ Karsog buses
From MandiAbout 99 km, around 3 h 40 min by car. Mandi ⇄ Karsog buses
From Chandigarh and DelhiAbout 217 km from Chandigarh (ISBT Sector 43), around 6 h by car; about 434 km from Delhi (Kashmere Gate ISBT), around 10 h. Karsog ⇄ Delhi bus
Nearest airportBhuntar (Kullu), about 150 km, around 4 h 45 min by car. All distances
Getting around

Taxis and local buses

TaxisTaxis wait at the Karsog taxi stand in the main market. Short local trips cost about ₹250 an hour, including the first 10 km. Agree the fare before you start.
Buses to the villagesHRTC and private buses leave Karsog bus stand for many villages of the valley, and for Shimla, Mandi and beyond. Every departure
Ready-made days

Day plans

Simple plans using local taxis, from the guides on this site. Fares are indicative; agree them before you start.

Temple dayMamleshwar Mahadev in the morning, then Kamaksha Devi at Kao, then Mahunag, back in Karsog by evening. A full day in a Dzire costs about ₹1,500–2,500.Mamleshwar · Kao · Mahunag
Hot springs and orchardsTattapani’s hot springs on the Sutlej (46 km) and the orchards and sunset views of Chindi on the way. Best from October to March.Tattapani · Chindi
Shikari Devi and JanjehliOver the Shikari Devi road to Janjehli (27 km, open about April to November), a night there, and the temple at first light, when the views are clearest.Shikari Devi · Janjehli
Kamru Nag trekDrive to Rohanda (52 km), then a 6 km forest trail to the sacred lake: 3–4 hours up, about 2 down. Start early.Kamru Nag
Where to stay

Hotels and homestays

Simple family-run hotels in Karsog town, the HPTDC Hotel Mamleshwar at Chindi (about 7 km), a PWD rest house and a few homestays. Book ahead in apple season and during big fairs.

When to go

The best season

March to November. May brings the Mahunag Mela, and the apple season (September–October) is especially beautiful. In winter (December–February) the higher roads can close for snow; July to September brings monsoon landslides.

FAQ

Common questions

How do I reach Karsog?
By road. Karsog is about 94 km from Shimla via Tattapani and about 99 km from Mandi, with HRTC and private buses from both. The nearest airport is Bhuntar (Kullu), about 150 km away.
What is the best time to visit Karsog?
March to November. May brings the Mahunag Mela, and the apple season (September–October) is especially beautiful. In winter the higher roads can close for snow.
How much does a taxi cost in Karsog?
Short local trips cost about ₹250 an hour, including the first 10 km. A full day of temple visits in a Dzire costs about ₹1,500–2,500. Agree the fare before you start.
