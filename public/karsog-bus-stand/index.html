FAQ

Common questions

What is the first bus from Karsog bus stand?
By the HRTC timetable board at Karsog bus stand (photographed 25 Sep 2026), the first departure is at 1:00 AM. Confirm at the stand, as the board may be out of date.
How many buses leave Karsog bus stand every day?
The HRTC board lists 49 departures a day on three lines: Karsog–Churag–Tattapani, Karsog–Pangna–Mandi and Karsog–Kelodhar. Private operators add about 20 more.
Is there a bus from Karsog to Delhi?
Yes. The HRTC board lists a Karsog–Delhi bus at 5:00 PM, and a Haridwar bus at 12:10 PM. Both can be booked on HRTC’s website.
Can I book Karsog buses online?
Some long-distance HRTC buses (Shimla, Mandi, Haridwar, Delhi) can be booked at online.hrtchp.com. Most local buses cannot; buy the ticket on the bus.
