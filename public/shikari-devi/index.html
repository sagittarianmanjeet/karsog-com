Temple on the peak

Shikari Devi

A goddess temple with no roof on the highest peak of Mandi district, 3,359 m up, with deodar forest all around and the snow ranges on the horizon. From Karsog, a mountain road climbs almost to the top.

3,359 m22 km from Karsog by roadOpen about April to November
Directions

The roofless temple

Shikari Devi sits on the top of a forested ridge between Karsog and Janjehli, at about 3,359 m, the highest point of Mandi district. On a clear day you can see range after range of the Himalaya from the top.

The shrine is famous for having no roof. Local people believe the goddess does not want one, and that every attempt to build a roof over her has failed. Many also say that snow, which lies deep on the peak all winter, never settles on the idol itself.

The legend

“Shikari” means hunter. By tradition, hunters prayed here before going into the forests below. Local people also believe the Pandavas worshipped the goddess on this peak during their exile.

How to reach

  • From Karsog: the Shikari Devi road climbs from Karsog to the temple: about 22 km, 1¼ hours by car on a narrow mountain road. Taxis are available at the Karsog taxi stand.
  • From Janjehli: a road of about 16–18 km climbs from Janjehli to the parking below the temple. Many people walk it, or take forest paths; ask locally.
  • The last part: from the parking, a steep flight of steps leads up to the temple. Take it slowly; you will feel the height.
  • In winter (about December to March) the peak is under snow and the roads are usually closed. Check the weather and ask in Karsog before you go.

When to go

April to June and September to November are best. July and August bring monsoon cloud, slippery roads and landslides. Navratri (March–April and September–October) brings pilgrims from across Mandi when the road is open.

Good to know

  • It is cold at the top even in summer. Carry a warm layer and rain protection.
  • Go early: mornings are clearest, and cloud often covers the peak by afternoon.
  • Mobile signal is weak on the mountain. Download offline maps and fill up with fuel in Karsog.
  • Carry water and snacks; shops near the top may only open in season.

FAQ

Common questions

How far is Shikari Devi from Karsog?
About 22 km by the Shikari Devi road, about 1¼ hours by car. The road is narrow and usually closed by snow in winter.
How high is Shikari Devi?
About 3,359 metres above sea level, the highest point of Mandi district.
Why does Shikari Devi temple have no roof?
Local people believe the goddess does not want a roof and that every attempt to build one has failed. Many also say that snow never settles on the idol, even in deep winter.
Can you drive to Shikari Devi temple?
Yes, in the open season. Roads from Karsog (about 22 km) and from Janjehli (about 16–18 km) reach a parking area below the temple; the last part is a steep flight of steps.
When is Shikari Devi open?
Usually from about April to November. In winter the peak is under snow and the roads are closed.
Where can I stay near Shikari Devi?
In Karsog town, or in Janjehli, which has small hotels, a PWD rest house and homestays. See hotels in Karsog.
