# Hindi on karsog.com — spellings and style

Every page of karsog.com has a Hindi copy at `/hi/…`. This page keeps the Hindi consistent.
Spellings follow the ones people in Karsog use most in local posts and news (counted in September 2026).

## Style

- Simple, everyday Hindi, the way people in Karsog talk and write (बोलचाल वाली सरल हिंदी). Avoid heavy Sanskrit words when a common word exists.
- Keep common English words that people use in Hindi: बस, टैक्सी, होटल, पार्किंग, ट्रेक, मैप, ऑनलाइन, बुकिंग.
- Address the reader as आप.
- Numbers in digits (0–9). Units: किमी, मीटर / मी, घंटे, मिनट, ₹.
- Times: सुबह 5:00 (4 AM–noon), दोपहर 1:30 (noon–4 PM), शाम 5:15 (4–7 PM), रात 9:00 (7 PM–4 AM).
- Dates: 15 सितंबर 2026. Months: जनवरी, फ़रवरी, मार्च, अप्रैल, मई, जून, जुलाई, अगस्त, सितंबर, अक्टूबर, नवंबर, दिसंबर.
- Beliefs and legends stay beliefs: "स्थानीय लोग मानते हैं…", "मान्यता है कि…". Never state a legend as fact.
- Same facts as the English page. Nothing added, nothing left out.
